## philosophy

### small containers

conversation happens when space forces it. seek out:
- 5-seat bars (can't ignore you)
- smoking areas (shared vice = instant bond)
- tachinomi (standing bars - no tables to hide behind)
- capsule hotel lounges
- train station benches during delays
- konbini parking lots at 3am

### hostels for common rooms

not just for budget. specific hostels with good social reputation vs sleep factories. Nui (Tokyo), Piece Hostel (Kyoto), The Dorm (Osaka). common rooms where people actually talk.

### participation > observation

**Matsuri you can actually join:**
- **Awa Odori (Tokushima)** - anyone can dance with the "fool's troupe" (niwaka-ren)
- **Nebuta (Aomori)** - rent a haneto costume, jump in
- **Yosakoi (Kochi/Sapporo)** - some teams take foreigners
- local shrine festivals - just ask. they often need people to carry the mikoshi

vs ones you watch: Gion, Takayama, most UNESCO-listed ones

## staying as the destination

### onsen towns

check in, put on yukata, spend the day going bath to bath. dinner at the ryokan. another bath. sleep. morning bath.

| Town | Region | Notes |
|------|--------|-------|
| **Kinosaki** | Kansai | 7 public baths, all tattoo-friendly. wander between them in yukata. the classic |
| **Arima** | Kobe | gold/silver water. main public baths tattoo-OK. day trip possible but overnight is the point |
| **Kurama** | Kyoto | mountain onsen, combine with hike to Kibune |
| **Ginzan** | Yamagata | wooden ryokan lining a river, gas lamps, snow. the Spirited Away one |
| **Nyuto** | Akita | remote mountain cluster, multiple baths, rustic. serious commitment to get here |
| **Beppu** | Kyushu | volcanic colored pools (jigoku), steam everywhere |
| **Dogo** | Shikoku | one of Japan's oldest, main bathhouse is 19th century |

### temple stays (shukubo)

**Koyasan** - the famous one. 51 temples offer lodging on a sacred mountain. shojin ryori (Buddhist vegetarian), morning prayers at 6am, walk Okunoin cemetery at night (200,000 gravestones in cedar forest). ¥10,000-40,000/night, book on Booking.com or temple sites

**Along Shikoku pilgrimage** - henro lodging. pilgrims eating together, sharing stories. different social context than tourism. temples 1-23 in Tokushima are accessible without doing the whole thing

**Elsewhere** - temples across Japan offer this, just less organized than Koyasan. search "shukubo" + region

### ryokan

the kaiseki dinner, the yukata, the futon on tatami, the pace of it. you're paying for the experience, not the room

- check-in around 3pm, check-out 10am. the schedule is the point
- dinner is the event - multi-course kaiseki, often in-room
- yukata to dinner, to the bath, to bed
- breakfast is also serious

¥15,000-50,000+ per person including meals. book direct or through Japanese sites (Jalan, Rakuten Travel) for better selection

### minshuku

family-run guesthouses. cheaper ryokan vibe, more personal. you're staying in someone's home, basically

- ¥5,000-10,000 including meals
- less formal than ryokan
- conversation with the family if you try
- often in rural areas, fishing villages, mountain towns

### farm stays (noka minshuku)

actual rural families hosting you. help with farm work (or don't), eat what they grow, see Japan that tourists never see

- **WWOOF Japan** - work exchange program, free stay for 4-6 hours work/day
- **Stay Japan** - booking platform for rural stays
- regional tourism programs (search prefecture + farm stay)

## regions

### Kanto

**Tokyo neighborhoods:**

**Akihabara**
ground level is tourist trap now (maid cafes, gacha). go vertical - upper floors still have weird trading card shops, modded Game Boys, niche electronics. Radio Kaikan rebuilt but upper floors still have stuff

**Nakano Broadway**
where the actual hobbyists went. 4 floors of vintage manga, old doujin, TYPE-MOON goods, weird military otaku shit. 26 Mandarake specialty shops (flagship). better than Akiba for finding things. also: watches (3F - Jackroad, Kamekichi, Firekids), cameras (Fujiya Camera)

**Koenji**
the new harajuku but grittier. birthplace of Japan's punk scene, anti-gentrification locals kept it unsanitized. comes alive at night - rotary outside station is where subcultures mix. Kitakore Building for experimental fashion (Hayatochiri - Lady Gaga visited). Earthdom for hardcore shows. vintage shops hidden upstairs in shotengai

**Shimokitazawa**
boho-chic version. narrow streets, vintage shops, live music venues. got more popular so slightly touristy now. CIRCULABLE SUPPLY, Notime (24hr thrift)

**Shibuya**
do the crossing once, get it out of your system. Shibuya Sky has the best observation deck (rooftop, not behind glass). Nonbei Yokocho - tiny bar alley behind station, older crowd, less English than Golden Gai, feels more real. Udagawacho backstreets for vintage/streetwear. Tower Records flagship is 9 floors. Disk Union has multiple specialty locations (jazz, punk, electronic - different buildings). Miyashita Park is a rooftop park on a mall now, weird but nice for sitting

**Shinjuku**
Golden Gai - 200+ tiny bars, 5-10 seats each. specific themes (death match bar, horror movie bar, noise music bar). some have cover (¥500-1500), some regulars-only. alive after 10pm. west exit has Camera Town

**Kabukicho**
touristy but still worth a walk-through at night. Godzilla head on TOHO building (8F terrace, free). Robot Restaurant closed but neon chaos is the point. late-night ramen like Fuunji nearby. host/hostess touts annoying but harmless - ignore them. more interesting after midnight

**Jimbocho**
hundreds of used bookstores. old manga, maps, academic texts, obscure shit

**Kichijoji**
Harmonica Yokocho (tiny bar alley). Nico vintage shop open til 11pm

**Takadanobaba**
AREA for visual kei shows (basement, 400 cap). Mikado game center for fighting games with old heads

**Yanaka**
traditional atmosphere, old Tokyo vibes. Yanaka Ginza shotengai

**Daikanyama**
architecture. also trash days in rich neighborhoods - people throw out perfect furniture/electronics

**Day trips from Tokyo:**
- **Kamakura** - beach + temples + hiking. 1hr from Tokyo. Great Buddha, Hase-dera, Zeniarai Benten (wash money for luck)
- **Nikko** - Toshogu shrine (ornate af), Kegon Falls, Lake Chuzenji. 2hr. can do day trip but overnight is better
- **Hakone** - onsen resort, Fuji views (weather permitting), open air museum, ropeway over volcanic valley
- **Chichibu** - night festival Dec 2-3, but also good for rural escape. ~2hr
- **Mashiko** - pottery town. studios everywhere, dig for your own clay. 2hr by train

**Yokohama** - Chinatown (Japan's largest), Sankeien garden, Cup Noodles Museum if you're weird

### Kansai

the other anchor. Kyoto, Osaka, Nara, Kobe, all within 30-60 min of each other

**Kyoto**
- **Architecture:** Kyoto International Conference Center (Metabolist brutalist), Kyoto Station (Hiroshi Hara steel canyon), Garden of Fine Arts (underground concrete)
- **Rock gardens:** Tofuku-ji Hojo (Shigemori Mirei, checker patterns), Daisen-in at Daitoku-ji, Komyo-in (empty, maple over gravel)
- **Hidden temples:** Honen-in, Murin-an, Gio-ji (moss garden), Shisen-do
- **Day trips:** Sanzen-in (Ohara), Takao temples, Enryaku-ji (Mt. Hiei)
- **Obvious:** Fushimi Inari (5am or accept crowds), Kinkaku-ji, Arashiyama bamboo, Gion/Pontocho

**Osaka**
- **Neighborhoods:** Shinsekai (grimey), Ura-Namba (alleys), Tenma/Fukushima (tachinomi), Den Den Town (otaku)
- **Music:** Namba Bears (legendary punk), King Cobra, Environment 0g (electronic/noise), Rug Time (jazz)
- **Food:** kushikatsu in Shinsekai, takoyaki, tachinomi in Tenma

**Nara** - deer, Kasuga Taisha (thousand lanterns), actually calmer than Kyoto

**Kobe**
- **Fate/ pilgrimage:** Kobe Bridge, Kitano-cho Ijinkan (Tohsaka house)
- **Hiking:** Mt. Rokko → Arima Onsen (perfect combo)
- **Sake:** Nada district breweries

**Small towns (Kansai):**
- **Omihachiman** - Edo merchant town, canal boat rides. uncrowded Kyoto alternative
- **Miyama** - thatched farmhouses in mountains. genuinely unchanged
- **Ine** - boathouses on water. unique

**Onsen:**
- **Kinosaki** - 7 public baths, ALL tattoo-friendly. wander in yukata. 2.5hr from Kyoto
- **Arima** - behind Kobe, gold/silver water. main baths tattoo-OK
- **Kurama** - north Kyoto, hike to Kibune after

### Chubu

the middle. mountains, crafts, preserved towns

**Kanazawa** - the actual Kyoto-without-crowds. Kenrokuen garden, Higashi Chaya geisha district, 21st Century Museum (contemporary art). Hokuriku Arch Pass to get here

**Takayama** - Japanese Alps, old merchant town, morning markets, sake breweries
- **Hida Furukawa** - 15 min away, even quieter

**Nagoya**
- what locals actually care about: **kissaten morning culture**. Komeda originated here. morning set culture is serious - coffee + toast + egg for the price of coffee. some places do it until 11am
- B-kyu gourmet: miso katsu, hitsumabushi (eel), tebasaki wings, ogura toast (red bean)
- **Osu** - electronics + traditional shops, Nagoya's Akiba

**Matsumoto** - castle town, gateway to Northern Alps

**Post towns (Kiso Valley):**
- **Tsumago & Narai** - best preserved Nakasendo. walk between them. this is the experience
- **Magome to Tsumago** - 8km hike through the past

**Craft:**
- **Takefu Knife Village (Fukui)** - 700 year knife-making history. same-day workshops
- **Wajima** - lacquerware, morning market run by grandmas for 1000+ years
- **Echizen** - washi paper

the north. fewer tourists, more hospitality

**Aomori**
- **Nebuta Matsuri** (early Aug) - massive illuminated floats, CAN JOIN as haneto
- Hirosaki Castle, apple country

**Akita**
- **Yokote Kamakura** (Feb) - snow huts

**Yamagata**
- Yamadera - 1000 steps to clifftop temple
- Ginzan Onsen - the one from Spirited Away vibes. wooden ryokan, snow, lanterns

**Sendai**
- Biggest city, gateway to Matsushima (bay with 260 islands)
- Gyutan (beef tongue) specialty

**Why Tohoku:**
- people are more welcoming, less used to foreigners
- cheaper
- actual wilderness
- regional dialects are wild

### San'in & Chugoku

west coast facing the Sea of Japan

**Matsue** - castle town, Lafcadio Hearn's house, canals, sunset on Shinji-ko lake
**Izumo Taisha** - one of Japan's oldest/most important shrines. where the gods meet in October
**Tottori Sand Dunes** - surreal. Japan's only desert-looking landscape

**Onomichi** - hillside port town, cats everywhere, tangled temple paths. ferry to Shimanami Kaido islands (cycling route to Shikoku)

**Hiroshima**
- Peace Memorial. you know
- Miyajima - floating torii gate, momiji manju, deer

**The inaka problem:** rural areas are incredible but need a car or local connection. trains thin out. consider car rental for San'in coast

### Shikoku

the fourth island. pilgrimage, valleys, art islands

**88 Temple Pilgrimage (Shikoku Henro)**
even a few temples changes the social context. white-clad pilgrims, stamp books, temple lodging
- do a section, not the whole thing (1200km takes 6 weeks walking)
- temples 1-23 are in Tokushima, accessible
- meet actual pilgrims

**Iya Valley** - remote mountain valley, vine bridges, thatched houses. Japan's forgotten interior

**Naoshima** - art island. Chichu Art Museum (Tadao Ando, Monet room), pumpkins, installation everywhere. but also: getting overrun, try Teshima or Inujima instead

**Tokushima** - Awa Odori in August (biggest dance festival, CAN JOIN with fool's troupe)

### Kyushu

southern island, volcanic, different vibe

**Fukuoka**
- Hakata ramen (tonkotsu), yatai (outdoor food stalls) along Nakasu river
- Gateway city, direct flights

**Nagasaki** - Dutch/Portuguese history, Peace Park, Oura Church (Fate/ Kotomine's church)

**Beppu** - hell tour (jigoku), onsen town with colored volcanic pools

**Yakushima** - ancient cedar forests (Jomon Sugi is 7000 years old), Princess Mononoke inspiration. requires ferry/flight. serious hiking

**Kagoshima** - Sakurajima volcano erupts constantly. ash falls on the city. wild

## practical

### money
cash still heavy. 7-Eleven ATMs work with foreign cards

### transport
- IC card (Suica/Pasmo) immediately
- JR Pass: went up 70% in 2023 (¥50k/7 days). only worth it for multi-city. must buy online before arriving. regional passes often better
- takkyubin: send luggage ahead via Yamato/Sagawa at any konbini. ¥1500-2500

### airports
- **Haneda** > Narita if you have a choice
- **Narita:** Skyliner (Ueno) or NEX (Shinjuku/Shibuya)
- **Kansai (KIX):** fly out to save final shinkansen

### apps
- Google Maps, Tabelog (3.5 = good), LINE, GO Taxi
- Suica in Apple/Google Wallet
- NERV (disaster alerts)

### accommodation
- Capsule: ¥3000-5000
- Manga cafe: ¥1500-2500 (missed train backup)
- Business hotel: ¥6000-12000 (Dormy Inn has onsen)
- Ryokan: ¥15000-50000+
